## 1) Recommended Free Turkish Instruments — Detailed

### 1.1 DSK World Stringz (Updated) — ⭐ PRIMARY FOR THIS PROJECT

- **Contains:** 13 world strings — **Turkish Oud, Kanun (listed as Kanum/Kanum), Cümbüş, Tar**, plus Celtic Harp, Dobro, Guzhen, Koto, Dulcimer, Sitar, Pipa, etc. (updated version expanded from original 8 to 13 instruments) [2](https://www.dskmusic.com/dsk-world-stringz/) [4](https://plugins4free.com/plugin/1085)
- **Quality:** Velocity response, amp envelope, micro-detuner, HP/LP filter, MIDI automation. 32.8 MB → 33.84 MB original pack, updated version same order. Reviewers praise tone difference across low/high notes and authenticity for free [3](https://rekkerd.org/dsk-music-releases-dsk-world-stringz/)
- **Format:** VST, VST3, AU (Windows & macOS, 32 & 64-bit)
- **License:** Free for private & commercial use — donation optional
- **Use in FL Studio:** Add as instrument channel → load → pick Turkish Oud, Kanun, Cümbüş, Tar
- **Link:** [DSK World Stringz Official](https://www.dskmusic.com/dsk-world-stringz-updated/) · also [original page](https://www.dskmusic.com/dsk-world-stringz/)

> This is the **only free VST that ships with Turkish Oud + Kanun + Cümbüş together** and runs without Kontakt — ideal as reference engine for synthesizing those timbres in the organ app.

### 1.2 EasternONE — Free Oriental Plugin (no Kontakt, 32-bit)

- **Contains:** Oud, Qanun, Ney, Kaman, Santoor, Mejwiz/Semsemia, Clarinet, Accordion + oriental scale tuning & fine-tune per note
- **Pros:** Free forever, small (86 MB), built-in oriental scale tuning
- **Cons:** 32-bit only, old (last update ~2010s), UI dated
- **Link:** [EasternONE on plugins4free](https://www.plugins4free.com/plugin/1085) variant; project page search “EasternONE Findasound”
- **Verdict:** Useful fallback if you need quick oriental palette without Kontakt, but UI and bit-depth limit professional use — kept for research completeness.

### 1.4 Raw Samples You Can Resynthesize (High-Purity, CC0/Sampling+)

- **VCSL — Versilian Community Sample Library:** Hundreds of orchestral & world samples, WAV + ready SFZ maps, CC0 licensed [FREE_MUSIC_LIBRARIES_AR.md]. Good for bowed strings, flutes, percussion base that you color with Turkish EQ/filter + microtuning to approximate Kemenche, Zurna beds.
- **VSCO2-CE — VS Chamber Orchestra Community Edition:** Chamber strings, brass, woodwinds, percussion — WAV + SFZ, CC0. Same as VCSL with overlap; pick one.
- **University of Iowa MIS:** Raw single-note recordings (violin, cello, flute, brass, piano, percussion) — most in **anechoic chamber, 24-bit/96 kHz stereo single notes** [FREE_MUSIC_LIBRARIES_AR.md]. Not pre-mapped chromatically; import into FL Sampler and spread across keyboard. Excellent for building your own Kanun / Ney multi-sample via FL Studio DirectWave.
- **FreePats:** Piano, guitar, violin, sax, flute, percussion — mixed SFZ/WAV/FLAC/SF2; check license per pack. World Percussion sub-page directly covers **Darbuka, Riq, Bendir** etc.
- **How to Turkish-ify them:** Load in Sforzando/sfizz → add pitch envelope, micro-detuner (-50 cents on chosen degrees), Turkish EQ (boost 800Hz for oud body, 1.2k for kanun shimmer), light plate reverb.

## 4) How “Tashriq” (Quarter-Tone / Maqam) Works — Without Needing Theory

Some Turkish/Arabic scales use notes **between piano keys** (quarter-tones, ~50 cents). Example: Rast needs Eb half-flat (≈ Eb -50c). Western equal temperament cannot play this.

**Solutions used in this project:**

- **Per-note micro-detuner:** The app can detune any pitch class (C, C#, D ...) by **-100 to +100 cents** in 1-cent steps, or quick buttons **-50 / +50 (quarter)** and **1/9–9/9 comma** (like Portable ORG’s 1/9 comma system [2](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.audiosdroid.portableorg&hl=en) [3](https://portable-org-keyboard.en.download.it/android)).
- **Maqam presets (20+):** Rast, Bayati, Hijaz, Saba, Nikriz, Hicaz, Kurd, Nahawand, Segah, Huzam, Acemasiran, Buselik, Ferahnak, Karcigar, Mahur, Neva, Suzinak, Sultani Yegah, Sehnaz, Uzzal, Zengule plus custom save/load — mirroring industry-standard ORG apps (Portable ORG lists 29 [4](https://playmods.net/apps/portable-org-keyboard/com.audiosdroid.portableorg)).
- **Live enable:** “MAQAM ON/OFF” glows amber; affected black+white keys tint purple/orange; pitch-bend wheel still works additively.
- **Reference tuning:** Master tune A = **392–452 Hz** (covers A=432, 440, 444 Turkish concert). Transpose ±12.
- **Implementation:** Web Audio `detune` on each voice oscillator + buffer source (cent = 1200·log2(f2/f1)). Applied per MIDI note before envelope, so recorded MIDI keeps microtuning.

> If you load external SFZ/SF2 samples (e.g., DSK or VCSL), the same microtuner applies — samples are pitch-shifted via `detune` cents, not retuned at source.

## 5) Can You Add More Instruments / Rhythms?

**Yes — three ways:**

1. **Load free SFZ/SF2/WAV** — Settings → Import → pick `.sfz`, `.sf2`, `.wav`, `.flac` → app auto-maps chromatically & assigns microtuning slot.
2. **Duplicate & edit preset** — Long-press any of the 10 instruments → Edit → change waveform, filter, envelope, then Save as “My Saz 2”.
3. **Add rhythm pattern** — Rhythm → Edit → drum grid (D/T/K) for Darbuka/Bendir/Riq → choose meter (2/4, 4/4, 5/8, 6/8, 7/8, 9/8) and Save.

All user additions are stored in IndexedDB / localStorage and survive app update (exportable as JSON).

## 6) Quality & Legal Notes

- **“High purity” ≠ one number:** Check bit depth (24-bit/48kHz typical in free packs), mic position, velocity layers, round robins, noise floor — not just 44.1 vs 48k. Salamander/Yamaha C5 16 layers vs VCSL few layers example [FREE_MUSIC_LIBRARIES_AR.md].
- **WAV vs FLAC:** Some packs (VCSL) offer FLAC lossless compressed; audible same as WAV, smaller. Verify per download.
- **Free ≠ redistributable:** All listed publishers allow *using the sound inside your music* but **forbid reselling/re-hosting the raw samples**. The organ app therefore **does not ship any copyrighted sample file** — it synthesizes timbres and optionally lets *you* load your own legally obtained samples. This keeps the APK legally shippable on Play Store.
- **Listen first:** Community packs (Pianobook, FreePats) vary widely — audition before relying in production.
- **Check disk:** Splice/Berlin/Spitfire packs require 240 MB → 23 GB depending on library. Plan storage.
